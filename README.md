## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm deeply passionate about **VLSI and ASIC design**. I actively work with **Verilog and SystemVerilog**, and I'm focused on building a strong foundation in **digital design, RTL development, and hardware architecture**.

My interests span across the full chip design flow — from **logic design and simulation to verification and timing-aware thinking**. Alongside VLSI, I also explore **embedded systems and low-level hardware-software integration**, which gives me a practical, system-level perspective on engineering.

I believe in learning by building, breaking, and optimizing — constantly pushing for cleaner RTL, better timing, and smarter architectures.
